# Privacy Policy for TaskMate Planner

**Last Updated: January 5, 2026**

## Introduction

TaskMate Planner ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Account Information
- Email address (for authentication)
- Display name (optional)
- Profile information (optional)

### Task Data
- Tasks, notes, and project information you create
- Due dates, priorities, and completion status
- Group and organization memberships
- Task assignments and collaborator information

### Subscription Information
- Subscription status and plan type
- Payment information is processed by Apple and RevenueCat (we do not store payment details)

### Automatically Collected Information
- Device information (model, OS version)
- App usage statistics (crash reports, performance data)
- IP address (for security purposes)

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Provide and maintain the TaskMate service
- Sync your tasks across devices
- Enable team collaboration features
- Process your subscription
- Send important service notifications
- Improve app performance and fix bugs
- Provide customer support

## Data Storage and Security

- Your data is stored securely using Supabase (cloud database provider)
- All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS
- Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text
- We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data

## Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

We share data only with:
- **Supabase** (database hosting - required for app functionality)
- **RevenueCat** (subscription management - only subscription status)
- **Apple** (authentication and payments)

We may disclose your information if required by law or to protect our rights.

## Team Collaboration

When you join an organization:
- Your display name and email become visible to other members of that organization
- Tasks you create or are assigned to are visible according to your role permissions
- Organization owners and admins can see your membership and role

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your account and all associated data
- Export your data
- Opt-out of non-essential communications

## Data Retention

- Active account data: Stored while your account is active
- Deleted accounts: Permanently deleted within 30 days
- Backup copies: Removed within 90 days

## Children's Privacy

TaskMate is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.
