## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I get started?
Download TaskMate from the App Store and create an account. You can start using personal tasks immediately for free!

### What's included in the free version?
The free version includes unlimited personal tasks with all core features: due dates, priorities, notes, recurring tasks, and offline sync.

### How do I upgrade to Pro?
Tap your profile icon → "Subscribe to Pro" → Choose your plan (Monthly or Annual)

### What do I get with TaskMate Pro?
Pro unlocks team collaboration features:
- Create organizations
- Invite unlimited team members
- Create groups and projects
- Assign tasks to team members
- Role-based permissions (Admin, Manager, Member)

### How do I create an organization?
After upgrading to Pro, tap the menu (top right) → "Create Organization" → Enter your organization name

### How do I invite team members?
1. Open your organization
2. Tap the menu → "Org Settings"
3. Create or select a group
4. Tap "Invite" and enter their email address

### How do I create a project?
1. Switch to the Projects tab
2. Tap the "+" button
3. Enter project name and select members

### My tasks aren't syncing
- Pull down to refresh on the Tasks or Projects screen
- Check your internet connection
- Make sure you're signed in (check profile icon)

### How do I manage my subscription?
Your subscription is managed through the App Store:
1. Open Settings app on your iPhone
2. Tap your name → Subscriptions
3. Find TaskMate Planner

### Can I transfer organization ownership?
Yes! Owners can transfer ownership to another team member:
1. Tap menu → "Org Settings"
2. Scroll to bottom → "Transfer Ownership"
3. Select the new owner

### How do I delete my account?
Email us at **support@taskmate-app.com** and we'll help you delete your account and all associated data.
